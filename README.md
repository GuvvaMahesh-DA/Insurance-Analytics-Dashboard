# 🛡️ Insurance Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The Insurance Analytics Dashboard is designed to analyze insurance business performance and provide actionable insights through interactive visualizations. The dashboard helps stakeholders monitor key metrics such as premium growth, policy performance, customer trends, and overall business growth.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze insurance policy performance.
- Track premium growth trends.
- Monitor customer and policy metrics.
- Identify business growth opportunities.
- Support data-driven decision-making.

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Premium Amount
- Premium Growth %
- Total Policies
- Active Customers
- Policy Renewal Rate
- Year-over-Year Growth
- Branch-wise Performance

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### Premium Analysis
- Total Premium Collected
- Premium Growth Trend
- Year-wise Premium Performance

### Policy Analysis
- Total Number of Policies
- Policy Distribution
- Policy Growth Analysis

### Customer Analysis
- Customer Count
- Customer Retention Insights
- Customer Segmentation

### Branch Performance
- Branch-wise Premium Contribution
- Top Performing Branches
- Regional Performance Comparison
